## Unit 1: The Joy of Teaching

### Section 1

1. "O My servants! My holy, My divinely ordained Revelation may be likened unto an ocean in whose depths are concealed innumerable pearls of great price, of surpassing luster. It is the duty of every seeker to bestir himself and strive to attain the shores of this ocean, so that he may, in proportion to the eagerness of his search and the efforts he hath exerted, partake of such benefits as have been preordained in God's irrevocable and hidden Tablets."
   — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), CLIII, par. 5, p. 369.
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

2. "The utterance of God is a lamp, whose light is these words: Ye are the fruits of one tree, and the leaves of one branch."
   — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), CXXXII, par. 3, p. 326. (resolved from "Ibid.")
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

3. "The best beloved of all things in My sight is Justice; turn not away therefrom if thou desirest Me, and neglect it not that I may confide in thee."
   — Bahá'u'lláh, *The Hidden Words* (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 2003, 2012 printing), Arabic no. 2, p. 3.
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

4. "Be anxiously concerned with the needs of the age ye live in, and center your deliberations on its exigencies and requirements."
   — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), CVI, par. 1, p. 241.
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

5. "All men have been created to carry forward an ever-advancing civilization."
   — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), CIX, par. 2, p. 243. (resolved from "Ibid.")
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

6. "The world passeth away, and that which is everlasting is the love of God."
   — Bahá'u'lláh, in *Women: Extracts from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh, 'Abdu'l-Bahá, Shoghi Effendi and the Universal House of Justice*, compiled by the Research Department of the Universal House of Justice (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1986, 1997 printing), no. 53, p. 26.
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

7. "Thou art My lamp and My light is in thee. Get thou from it thy radiance and seek none other than Me. For I have created thee rich and have bountifully shed My favor upon thee."
   — *The Hidden Words* (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 2003, 2012 printing), Arabic no. 11, p. 6.
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

### Section 3

8. "O My servants! The one true God is My witness! This most great, this fathomless and surging Ocean is near, astonishingly near, unto you. Behold it is closer to you than your life-vein! Swift as the twinkling of an eye ye can, if ye but wish it, reach and partake of this imperishable favor, this God-given grace, this incorruptible gift, this most potent and unspeakably glorious bounty."
   — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), CLIII, par. 5, p. 370.
   — Designation: Reflection quote

### Section 4

9. "O wayfarer in the path of God! Take thou thy portion of the ocean of His grace, and deprive not thyself of the things that lie hidden in its depths. Be thou of them that have partaken of its treasures. A dewdrop out of this ocean would, if shed upon all that are in the heavens and on the earth, suffice to enrich them with the bounty of God, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. With the hands of renunciation draw forth from its life-giving waters, and sprinkle therewith all created things, that they may be cleansed from all man-made limitations and may approach the mighty seat of God, this hallowed and resplendent Spot."
   — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), CXXIX, par. 1, p. 316. (resolved from "Ibid.")
   — Designation: Quotation to Memorize

### Section 5

10. "The Word of God may be likened unto a sapling, whose roots have been implanted in the hearts of men. It is incumbent upon you to foster its growth through the living waters of wisdom, of sanctified and holy words, so that its root may become firmly fixed and its branches may spread out as high as the heavens and beyond."
    — Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (Wilmette: Bahá'í Publishing Trust, 1983, 2017 printing), XLIII, par. 9, p. 109. (resolved from "Ibid.")
    — Designation: Reflection quote

### Section 5

4. "They who are the beloved of God, in whatever place they gather and whomsoever they may meet, must evince, in their attitude towards God, and in the manner of their celebration of His praise and glory, such humility and submissiveness that every atom of the dust beneath their feet may attest the depth of their devotion. The conversation carried by these holy souls should be informed with such power that these same atoms of dust will be thrilled by its influence. They should conduct themselves in such manner that the earth upon which they tread may never be allowed to address to them such words as these: 'I am to be preferred above you. For witness, how patient I am in bearing the burden which the husbandman layeth upon me. ## Notes on extraction

- Quotations are ordered by section, in the order they appear in the book.
- Section headers reflect the unit's internal section numbering, not a running count. Sections with no bolded, footnoted quotation (Unit 1 Section 2; Unit 2 Section 1; Unit 3 Sections 1, 2, 4, 9, 10, 11, 16) are omitted from this file, since they contained only exercise text, narrative, or unfootnoted quotes.
- Where the PDF's inline reference number pointed to "Ibid." this file resolves it to the full citation from the immediately preceding numbered reference. Two double-Ibid chains occur (Unit 3, references 6 and 7, both resolving back to reference 5) and are noted individually against each entry.
- Two quotations appear in the narrative dialogue of Unit 3, Section 10 (attributed to "the Bahá'í writings" and described as memorized by the character Beatrice: one beginning "Man's life has its springtime..." and one beginning "Material civilization is like unto the lamp...") but carry no footnote marker and are not resolvable against the unit's reference list. Per the skill's scope, they were left out of this extraction rather than guessed at.
- This file captures quotations, their resolved citations, and a Quotation to Memorize / Reflection quote designation. It does not match quotes to passage records in a separate prayers/passages corpus (e.g. `passage_id` for the `ruhi_quotations` table). That remains a separate, still-needed pass if this feeds the memorisation app's Ruhi mapping (see v4.0 scope doc, section 5).

### How the designation was tagged

A quote (or the run of quotes it introduces) is tagged **Quotation to Memorize** only
where the surrounding text gives an explicit, literal instruction to memorize it
("memorize", "memorization", "memorized", "committed to memory," or equivalent).
Everything else is tagged **Reflection quote**. This is a mechanical rule, applied
consistently rather than judging book intent quote by quote, so two places where the
book's language gestures at memorisation without using the word are flagged here
instead of decided silently:

- **Unit 3, Section 3, quotes 1–3.** The section closes with "Learning the quotations
  well is especially important, for sharing passages from the Writings in discussions
  of this kind is indispensable." This is a strong emphasis on internalising the
  passages but does not use "memorize." Tagged Reflection quote here; worth your own
  read if you're deciding what belongs in the memorisation corpus.
- **Unit 3, Section 6, quote 10.** Introduced with "Let these words be engraved on our
  minds and hearts," which is evocative but likewise not a literal memorize instruction.
  Tagged Reflection quote for the same reason.
